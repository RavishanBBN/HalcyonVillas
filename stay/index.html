              The house manual

              Arriving and leaving

              • Check-in is from 14:00. If you arrive earlier, leave your bags with us and head to the beach.
              • The house is at 17/09 Waulagoda Middle Road, a quiet lane off Galle Road. Tuk-tuk drivers know Waulagoda.
              • Check-out is by 11:00. Ask on your stay page if you need a little longer.

              The house

              • Please take off sandy shoes at the door; there is a tap by the gate for your feet.
              • Air conditioning works best with the doors closed. Switch it off when you go out.
              • Quiet hours are from 11:30 pm to 4:00 am, for the neighbours and the birds.
              • No smoking inside the property. The garden is yours.
              • Drinking water: please use the bottled water provided, not the tap.

              The sea

              • Swim only between the flags at Hikkaduwa main beach; currents are strong elsewhere, especially from May to October.
              • At the reef, look at the turtles, but please do not touch or feed them.
              • The sun is strong: reef-safe sunscreen, a hat and lots of water.

              Our favourite things to do

              • SunriseHikkaduwa Lake at dawn: kingfishers, herons and monitor lizards.
              • SnorkellingHire a mask at the main beach and swim out to the coral garden.
              • KayakingPaddle calm water at your own pace. Ask us and we will arrange it.
              • DivingReef dives with a local dive centre. Tell us if you are certified.
              • SurfingHikkaduwa has waves for every level. Boards and lessons on the main beach.
              • Galle FortHalf a day of ramparts, lanes and the lighthouse. Go in the late afternoon.
              • Whale watchingFrom Mirissa, December to April. Leave very early.
              • Rice and curryAsk us for our favourite local lunch places.
