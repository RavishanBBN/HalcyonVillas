Hikkaduwa

Out of the gate,
down to the sea.

The lane from our gate crosses Galle Road and ends on the sand. Everything else Hikkaduwa is famous for is a short tuk-tuk ride away.

  1. 01 Hikkaduwa Beach 100 m · 2 min walk Surf, sunsets and the reef.
  2. 02 Coral reef & turtles 100 m · 2 min walk Snorkel the shallow reef where sea turtles feed.
  3. 03 Narigama Beach 1.3 km · 5 min by tuk-tuk Long sand, beach cafés, the evening crowd.
  4. 04 Hikkaduwa Lake ≈ 2 km · 10 min by tuk-tuk Birdlife at dawn. Look out for kingfishers.
  5. 05 Galle Fort ≈ 18 km · 30 min by car Ramparts, lighthouse and old streets.
  6. 06 Madu River ≈ 25 km · 40 min by car Mangrove boat rides and island temples.

    Good to know

    Questions, answered.

    Is my booking confirmed when I send a request?

    Not yet. Your request is pending while we check the dates against every channel we take bookings from. We then email you a confirmation (or suggest other dates), usually within a few hours. Nothing is charged online.

    How do I pay?

    Once your stay is confirmed we share the payment details with you directly. Booking direct means no booking-site fees on our side.

    What are the check-in and check-out times?

    Check-in is from 14:00 and check-out is by 11:00. Arriving earlier or leaving later? Tell us in your request and we will do our best.

    Is breakfast included?

    Yes. Our prices include breakfast, Sri Lankan or Western, served from 7:00 to 10:00. Prefer to go without? Choose room only when you send your request.

    Is there anything for longer stays?

    Yes. Stay 3 nights or more and book here, and your airport pickup and drop-off are 25% off.

    Do you thank guests who recommend you?

    Yes. After you book, your stay page has a link to share. When a friend books with it, you both get 30% off kayaking, and you choose a thank-you: 25% off an airport drop, a free barbecue night, or 20% off your next stay.

    Can we book the whole villa?

    Yes. Choose “All of Halcyon” to have all three floors and the garden to yourselves, for up to 11 guests.

    Are children welcome?

    Yes, in the Garden Floor and the Balcony Floor (up to three children each). The Sky Room is for adults only.

    Is there parking and Wi-Fi?

    Free private parking at the house and free Wi-Fi throughout.

    Can I bring my pet?

    Sorry, pets are not allowed at Halcyon Rest.
