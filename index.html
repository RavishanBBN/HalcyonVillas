The house

Halcyon Rest is a private house on a quiet lane above Hikkaduwa Beach. Three residences, one on each floor. A garden to come home to. And the Indian Ocean, just 100 metres away.

Chapter I · The residences

One house, three ways to stay.

Each floor is a home of its own, with the garden, the kitchen or the balcony that makes it yours. Or take every floor, for up to 11 guests.

  1. Ground floor The Garden Floor Doors open to the garden. LKR 18,500 a night for two, breakfast included
  2. First floor The Balcony Floor A balcony above the palms. LKR 18,500 a night for two, breakfast included
  3. Second floor The Sky Room One room, at the top of the house. LKR 12,000 a night for two, breakfast included
  4. All three floors All of Halcyon The house, the garden and the quiet, all yours. Price on request

A day at Halcyon

Sunrise to starlight.

  1. 06:15

    First light

    The garden wakes before you do. Coffee on the step, birdsong in the palms.

  2. 08:00

    Down the lane

    A hundred metres down the lane and you are standing in the Indian Ocean.

  3. 11:00

    The reef

    Snorkel the shallows off Hikkaduwa, where sea turtles come in to feed.

  4. 15:00

    Slow afternoon

    Back in the cool of your room, or a long lunch of rice and curry in town.

  5. 18:10

    Sunset

    The sky turns coral over Narigama. Everyone on the beach goes quiet.

  6. 21:00

    Halcyon night

    The house is still. The sea is just loud enough to fall asleep to.
